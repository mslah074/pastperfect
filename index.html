Presentation

Learn the Past Perfect Tense step by step

🌟 The Past Perfect is used to talk about an action that happened BEFORE another past action or time.

Example: She had left when he arrived.
👉 "had left" happened FIRST — "arrived" happened SECOND

🎯 Concept Checking Questions (CCQs)

Tap each question to reveal the expected answer 👇

1
Are we talking about the past?
✅ Yes! Both actions happened in the past.
2
How many actions are there in the past?
✅ Two actions — "she left" and "he arrived".
3
Did both actions happen at the same time?
❌ No! They happened at different times.
4
So did one action happen before the other?
✅ Yes! One action happened before the other.
5
Which action happened first?
✅ "She had left" happened first (Past Perfect). Then he arrived (Past Simple).
Subject + had + Past Participle (V3)

✅ She had left when he arrived.

✅ After I had finished my homework, I went to bed.

Subject + hadn't + Past Participle (V3)

❌ We hadn't met him before the party.

❌ She hadn't finished her work before he called.

Had + Subject + Past Participle (V3) ?

🔵 Had Nada come before you?

→ Yes, she had.  /  No, she hadn't.

Q. Word + had + Subject + Past Participle (V3) ?

🟣 How had you solved the problem before class?

→ I had asked for my teacher's help.

💡 Remember: Past Simple vs Past Perfect

PAST SIMPLE

Actions that started & ended in the past.
He arrived yesterday.

PAST PERFECT

Action before ANOTHER past action.
She had left when he arrived.
